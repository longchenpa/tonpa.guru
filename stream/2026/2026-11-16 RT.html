RT

Fast Clean-room Multiplatform C Run-Time for RTOS

I put together a small clean-room C99 runtime focused on real-time and high-integrity constraints:

— Zero heap requirement, no large static tables (no Unicode/collation).

— O(1) bounded stack sort.

— Designed against MISRA C:2023 / CERT C / NASA Power of Ten style rules.

— Current size: ~1.2–10.5 kB .text depending on configuration.

— Explicit comparisons against dietlibc, picolibc, NuttX libc, etc.

Page with details and size tables: rt.btron.pro

Curious about feedback from people who actually ship constrained or safety-oriented RTOS code. What would you change or drop?
